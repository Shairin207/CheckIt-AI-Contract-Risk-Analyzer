## Overview

**CheckIt** is an AI-powered employment contract risk analysis platform designed to help users understand complex employment agreements through intelligent document analysis. The system uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to identify risky contract clauses, simplify legal terms, detect potential labour-rights concerns, generate contract risk scores, and produce downloadable analysis reports.

Employment contracts often contain complex legal language that can be difficult for job seekers, employees, freelancers, and fresh graduates to understand. As a result, many individuals sign agreements without fully understanding their rights, responsibilities, and possible risks. CheckIt addresses this challenge by providing an accessible AI-assisted decision-support platform for employment contract understanding.

The system is designed as an informational tool to improve contract transparency and awareness. It does not replace professional legal consultation.

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of CheckIt are:

- Develop a secure AI-powered employment contract analysis platform.

# Key Features

## AI-Based Contract Analysis

Users can upload employment contracts and receive automated AI-powered analysis.

## Clause Extraction

The system extracts important sections and clauses from employment agreements for further analysis.

## Risk Detection

CheckIt identifies potentially risky contract elements, including:

- Termination clauses
- Confidentiality agreements
- Non-compete clauses
- Salary and benefits conditions
- Employment obligations
- Employee rights and responsibilities

## Simplified Clause Explanation

The platform converts complex legal language into easier explanations so that non-legal users can understand contract conditions.

## Labour Rights Detection

The system identifies clauses that may create concerns related to fairness, employee rights, and workplace practices.

## Contract Risk Score

The platform generates an overall risk score to help users quickly understand the safety level of an employment agreement.

## Automated Reports

Users can generate downloadable contract analysis reports containing identified risks, explanations, and insights.

# Problem Statement

Employment agreements frequently contain complicated legal terms that are difficult for individuals without legal knowledge to understand. Many people sign contracts without knowing the implications of clauses related to obligations, termination, confidentiality, restrictions, and employee rights.

This can result in:

- Uninformed contract decisions
- Increased legal and financial risks
- Limited awareness of labour rights
- Dependence on expensive legal consultations
- Reduced confidence during employment decisions

CheckIt aims to reduce these challenges by providing AI-assisted contract understanding.

# Project Architecture

The CheckIt platform consists of several major components:

### Frontend Layer

Provides user interaction through a web interface where users can upload contracts and view analysis results.

### Backend Layer

Handles authentication, API communication, business logic, and data management.

### AI/NLP Layer

Processes contract documents, extracts clauses, classifies risks, and generates explanations.

### Database Layer

Stores user information, contract analysis history, and system records.

### Reporting Layer

Generates downloadable reports containing contract insights and risk assessments.
